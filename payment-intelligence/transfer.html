Transfer (SNAP)

An intrabank transfer sent the way a partner bank would send it: as a SNAP Transfer Intrabank request, signed with SHA256withRSA and encrypted with RSA-OAEP + AES-256-GCM. Boomi verifies and decrypts it, then Payment Intelligence Risk Analyst (the AI agent) and the deterministic rules decide whether the transfer goes through, is held for review or is blocked. The response comes back encrypted and signed too.

Demo scenarios

Signing and encryption happen in the portal's server, which acts as the partner bank's backend. Private keys never reach the browser.
